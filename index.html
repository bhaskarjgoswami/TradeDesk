Charting process

  1. Mark the higher-timeframe range and premium / discount.
  2. Mark liquidity: previous-day highs / lows and equal highs / lows.
  3. Decide the context: continuation, pullback or reversal.
  4. Pick one target: opposing zone or structure.
  5. Define invalidation: “My bias is wrong if price breaks and holds beyond…”

Trade management rules

Predefine risk before entry: fixed R, no resizing mid-trade.
Set and forget.
If invalidation hits, exit and reassess. No “one more trade to make it back.”

Trading notes

  • Bias is a plan and invalidation, not a prediction.
  • If you cannot say your bias in one sentence, you do not have one.
  • If invalidation hits: reset. No coping, no revenge trade.
  • Build trust in your system with data.
  • Conviction → confidence → competence → consistency.
